# বাংলা শিখি: plan for the free course (and the premium one later)

Status: **approved 2026-11-27; phases 1–3 built (D321, D322).** Premium (phase 4) waits for the owner: prices,
payment and paid features are the owner's decision (CLAUDE.md §3). Sound: human recordings uploaded at the desk.
Also built: English instructions and an English beginner's guide for parents and learners who do not read Bengali.
Written with: a learning designer, a market researcher, a business/marketing analyst and the UX review of the first
version (D317).

---

## 1. What is wrong with today's pages (the owner was right)

1. **They are reference sheets, not lessons.** কার is 10 signs × 3 words on one 5,500px scroll; যুক্তাক্ষর is a list of 39.
   Nothing asks the learner to *do* anything, so nobody knows if they learnt it.
2. **The বর্ণমালা page says "tap to hear"**, but no audio is approved yet, so every tap does nothing.
3. **50 letters in dictionary order.** Children learn faster from a few common letters that make real words at once
   (মা, বাবা, কাক).
4. **The jumps are too big**: all 10 কার at once, then 39 conjuncts. Blending (ক + া → কা → কাক), the core of reading,
   is never practised.
5. **Nothing to read.** No short stories made only of letters already learnt.
6. **No progress**, no "where was I", no stars.
7. **Parents who read little Bengali get three bullet points**, not a script for each lesson.
8. **Grown-up buttons on a child's screen**: "সাইন ইন" and the red "+ লিখুন" are the strongest buttons on the page.
9. **Adults learning again are not served** (no faster track, no note on the inherent vowel: কলম is "kolom").
10. **Worksheets are copy boxes only**, no stroke order, no picture, letters spill out on phones, and there is no real
    PDF to download.

## 2. What the market says

- **No good free path exists.** Duolingo has no Bengali course. Ling ($80–90/year) is criticised for paywalling early.
  Children's alphabet apps (millions of downloads) stop at the 50 letters. BALA Bornomala (free, US heritage school) is
  the closest; Google Read Along is the free benchmark for reading practice.
- **Families pay for tutors**: Preply ~$13 a lesson, Outschool ~$15 a class; weekend schools are free to $5/month.
- **What parents ask for**: a structured path, help when they cannot read the script themselves, real reading (not just
  letters), printables, and both Bengals' words (জল/পানি, ঠাকুমা/দাদি). No app handles both Bengals.
- **Worksheet search results are poor** ("bengali alphabet worksheet pdf" gives spammy pages): a clear opening for us.
- **Gaps we can own**: literature-quality graded stories with every poet credited; both Bengals; good free printables;
  depth after the alphabet (কার, ফলা, যুক্তাক্ষর); a parent-helper mode; no ads and no early paywall for children.

## 3. The promise

- **বাংলায়:** «অক্ষর থেকে প্রথম গল্প: বাংলা পড়া শিখুক আপনার সন্তান, বিনা খরচে।»
- **English:** "From first letter to first story: your child reads Bengali, free, from a magazine that has published
  Bengali literature since 2012."

Who it is for, in order: (1) parents abroad with children aged 4–10, (2) weekend Bengali schools and puja committees
abroad, (3) English-medium families in Kolkata and Dhaka, (4) adults learning to read again. It welcomes both Bengals
and every faith (many UK families are Sylheti-speaking Muslims): no Hindu-festival-only framing.

## 4. The free course: the learner's path

Same units for everyone; three ways through:
**child abroad** (parent helper on, Roman and English shown), **child in Bengal** (school order, fewer English lines),
**adult** (Roman on, no stickers, can start with a short placement check).

| Unit | What | Ends with |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Getting started: who is learning, is a parent helping | — |
| 1 | First 8 letters: ম ব ক ল ন প ত + া | reading মা, বাবা, কাকা, মামা, তাল |
| 2–4 | More consonants, 8 at a time, common first; look-alikes (ব/র, ড/ড়, ত/ৎ, য/ষ) taught as pairs | 10 words per unit |
| 5 | The hidden vowel and blending: কল, বন, কলম | reading 3-letter words |
| 6–8 | কার two or three at a time: া ি ী → ু ূ ে → ো ৈ ৌ ৃ; the বারোখড়ি chart becomes a game here | 10 words per unit |
| 9 | Vowel letters at the start of words (আম, ইট, উট, এক); ং ঃ ঁ ৎ | — |
| 10 | ফলা and রেফ | — |
| 11–12 | যুক্তাক্ষর in three tiers: easy-to-see (স্ত, ন্ত, ন্দ), changed shape (ক্ষ, জ্ঞ, ঙ্গ, ত্র), rare (reference only) | — |
| 13 | Short sentences, only from letters already learnt | — |
| 14 | Five short stories (40–80 words) we write for learners, then a door to ছোটদের হাতপাখা | "I read a story!" |

About 60–70 lessons of 5–8 minutes. **The whole path is free forever** and is enough to read simple words, sentences
and short stories. A test makes sure every word in a lesson uses only letters already taught.

## 5. One lesson on a phone

A full-screen **learning mode**: no site menu, no bottom bar, no sign-in, no ads. At the top: ✕ (back to the map),
progress dots, stars. One thing per screen, about 8 screens:

1. **Meet**: the new letter, very large, with its picture; tap for the word, tap again for Roman + English.
2. **Watch it join**: ক + া slide together into কা.
3. **Trace once** with a finger (dotted guide, start dot).
4. **Practise**: 4–6 quick items, new and old letters mixed.
5. **Read it aloud**: 3 words or a sentence; the parent taps "পড়তে পেরেছে ✓" or "আবার দেখি".
6. **Small quiz**: 5 items, no timer, no lives; a wrong answer shows the right one and comes back once.
7. **Stars and sticker**, then "পরের পাঠ", "ওয়ার্কশিট", "একটা গল্প পড়ো".

A **"বাবা-মায়ের জন্য"** button on every screen opens a short script ("point, say 'ma', ask…") and pronunciation notes.
Big buttons (56px), Bengali at 28px or more, Roman/English switch remembered, works by tapping (drag optional),
respects reduced motion, never colour alone for right/wrong.

**Practice types that need no sound and no Bengali typing**: picture → word, word → picture, find the letter, odd one
out among look-alikes, build the word from tiles, add the right কার, split or join a যুক্তাক্ষর, trace, read aloud with a
parent, sentence → picture, one question after a story.

## 6. Progress without accounts

- Saved **on the device only** (nothing sent to us, no account for children). Up to 4 children per device, each just a
  name and an animal picture.
- A **map** of units with the current lesson glowing; "চালিয়ে যাও" opens exactly where they stopped.
- **Stars** per lesson, a **sticker** per unit from a Bengali set (ইলিশ, দোয়েল, আলপনা, হাতপাখা…) in a sticker book.
  A gentle "আজ একটা পাঠ", no streak pressure for children.
- Each lesson starts with 2 items the child missed before (quiet review).
- **Parent view** (behind a simple sum so children don't wander in): letters known / still shaky, minutes this week,
  "what to do tonight", a printable progress card, and a code/QR to move progress to another device by hand.

## 7. Printing and PDFs (done properly)

The web server cannot shape Bengali text into a PDF, so the **PDFs are made by the deploy job in a real browser**
(the same way the marketing studio draws cards, D182) from print pages, and saved as files on the site. Every PDF:

- comes in **A4 and US Letter** (families in America print on Letter);
- prints cleanly in black and white, with a মাত্রা guide line, stroke order and a start dot for each letter,
  a picture, then grey letters to write over and empty boxes;
- carries the lesson name, "hatpakha.com/janun/learn-bengali/" and the ISSN in the footer, nothing else.

What is printable, all free:
- one **worksheet per lesson**;
- an **alphabet chart** and a **কার chart** for the wall;
- a **teacher's set** per unit for weekend schools (all worksheets of the unit in one PDF, with an answer page);
- **certificate of completion** per unit, filled with the child's name on the device and printed (no data sent).

Downloads are counted on the server as a daily total per file (no cookies, no IP kept), which is how we measure without
analytics on children's pages. Worksheet *preview pages* are indexable (title, picture, what it practises) so
"bengali alphabet worksheet pdf" searches land on us; the print pages stay noindex.

## 8. Premium later (owner decides price and payment)

Premium is **more**, never **the rest of the path**:

| Premium | Why people pay |
|---|---|
| Illustrated graded readers (30–60 small books) with read-along | real reading, our own literature |
| Audio for every word and sentence, slow mode | parents who cannot read the script |
| Family account: progress on every device, several children | convenience |
| Level workbooks (40–80 pages per level), later a printed book | offline practice |
| Small live group classes | a teacher |
| Classroom pack for weekend schools (teacher view, bulk sheets) | schools abroad |

**Shown only in the parent view**, at three moments: a unit finished, the first story finished, a second child added.
Never inside a child's lesson.

**Price ranges found (hypotheses for the owner, not decisions):**
abroad $35–59 a year per family (£30–45); India ₹699–1,499 a year; Bangladesh ৳1,000–2,000 a year; one-off workbook
PDF $5–8 / ₹49–99; school pack $60–150 a year. Payment would need cards and Apple/Google Pay abroad, UPI in India and
bKash in Bangladesh; the vendor is a separate decision.

**Test before building**: a "জানিয়ে দিন" waitlist per premium feature in the parent view; the first paid item would be
the level workbook PDF (cheap to make from the worksheets).

## 9. How people will find it

- **Search**: English pages for "bengali alphabet worksheet pdf", "learn bengali for kids", "bengali kar chart",
  "bengali conjunct letters"; Bengali pages for "যুক্তাক্ষর তালিকা", "যুক্তবর্ণ", "বারোখড়ি", "কার চিহ্ন",
  "বাংলা বর্ণমালা লেখা অনুশীলন".
- **Weekend schools and puja committees abroad**: a list of 100; a personal note with the free teacher's set
  (with the puja booklet, D302); Saraswati Puja (হাতেখড়ি) in January–February, 21 February and পয়লা বৈশাখ for
  Bangladeshi associations. *Needs the owner's OK before any bulk e-mail.*
- **Parents' WhatsApp and Facebook groups** by city: one useful worksheet a week, not a link dump.
- **Pinterest** board per unit (tall worksheet pins); **Reels/Shorts** of a letter drawn stroke by stroke
  (no children on camera). *Opening new accounts needs the owner's OK.*
- **Into the magazine**: finished learners go to ছোটদের হাতপাখা; parents join the newsletter ("পরিবারের চিঠি":
  a weekly worksheet and a children's story, on the existing list); a call for children's stories written for learners.

## 10. Child safety and privacy

No account, analytics, ads or outside scripts on children's pages (D101). Progress stays on the device. Accounts and
payments, later, only on the parent pages, holding first names or nicknames only, reviewed under docs/SECURITY.md
(COPPA, UK Age Appropriate Design Code). Download counters keep no IP. No invented testimonials.

## 11. Build order

**Phase 1: fix what is there (about a week)**
- বর্ণমালা: honest text while there is no audio; tapping a card turns it to the word/picture instead of doing nothing.
- No bottom bar, no "লিখুন"/"সাইন ইন" on children's pages.
- Larger, darker Roman/English text; কার and যুক্তাক্ষর split into short steps; the বারোখড়ি game at the top.
- Worksheets fixed on phones; "start here" and a three-line parent script per lesson; the hidden-vowel note.
- Content corrections from the language check.

**Phase 2: the learning mode and units 1–5**
- One lesson player (plain JavaScript, no new packages) reading the units from a content file.
- Practice types 1–6 and read-aloud; map, stars, parent sheet; worksheet per lesson as real A4/Letter PDFs
  from the deploy job; download counter.
- The "only letters already taught" test.
- Owner-approved recorded or machine voice for the letters (labelled যন্ত্রকণ্ঠ, D103), if the owner wants sound now.

**Phase 3: the rest of the free path**
- Units 6–14, tracing, conjunct split/join, the five stories, sticker book, parent progress page, device-move code,
  teacher's sets and certificates; ছোটদের pieces tagged with a reading level so each unit can say "এখন পড়ো: …".
- Outreach to schools and parent groups starts with units 1–5 live.

**Phase 4: premium (only after the owner decides)**
- Waitlist results → price → payment vendor → family account, graded readers, full audio, workbooks, classes.
