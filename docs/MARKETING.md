# Marketing plan: from "announce the piece" to a reason to come every day

Status: **proposal for the owner (2026-11-13). Nothing here is built until approved.**
Replaces the current marketing agent's only job (one announcement with a link per new piece).

## 1. What the evidence says

Checked against projects that grew the way Hatpakha wants to. Figures in the sources vary and many are from
marketing blogs, so these are directions, not promises.

| Who | What worked | What we take |
|---|---|---|
| **Wordle / NYT Games** | One puzzle a day for everyone, and a result grid people could post without spoiling the answer. Players invented the grid; the game adopted it. | Players are the media. Every game result, medal and পাঠকের কলম line must be one tap from a share. |
| **Terribly Tiny Tales** (India) | Started as a Facebook page of tweet-sized tales set as an image (white text on black). The post *was* the story. Grew to a large community, then to longer formats. | The value sits in the post itself, as a recognisable card. The link is second. |
| **Rekhta** (Urdu) | A word a day with meaning and usage; poetry as cards; a festival as the yearly peak. Over a million Instagram followers. | A daily word and a daily line are habits a language community keeps. We already have 1,002 words with clues. |
| **Duolingo** | The "streak-saver" reminder was its first big retention win; wording mattered more than frequency; a game mechanic copied without fitting the product did nothing. | One evening reminder to people about to lose a streak. Test the wording. No mechanics for their own sake. |
| **Morning Brew** | A referral programme with milestone rewards (3, 5, 10 friends…) and a share block in every issue brought a large share of new subscribers. It worked because people already shared the content. | Invite milestones with rewards that cost nothing: a medal, a day-early serial episode, a name on the friends' list. |
| **Pratilipi** | Writers first, then readers; most new writers start as readers; reader and writer groups. | Writers are our distribution. পাঠকের কলম is the bridge from reader to writer. |
| **News publishers on WhatsApp Channels** | Channels grew with little promotion; one daily digest of the top items is the common format. Metrics are thin and posting is manual. | WhatsApp is where our readers are. One message a day, written for us, pasted by a person. |
| **Facebook / Instagram ranking** | Posts with links get a small share of views; comments, shares and saves count more than likes; Reels reach furthest; carousels are saved and engaged with most. | Picture or carousel first, link in the first comment or the caption's end. Ask a real question. |

Sources are listed at the end.

## 2. The strategy in one line

**Give something complete in every post, give people a reason to come daily, and make sharing the easy thing.**
Three loops, each feeding the next:

1. **Discover** (social): a card worth stopping for → some tap through.
2. **Return** (site, phone, e-mail, WhatsApp): a daily habit: game, riddle, serial, where-you-stopped.
3. **Share** (readers and writers): results, lines, medals, their own published words → new people discover.

## 3. Nine plays

### Play 1. The Hatpakha card (signature format)
One look for everything we post: cream card, the fan, one thought. Recognisable in a feed without reading the name.
- **এক লাইনের হাতপাখা** (daily): the strongest line of a piece, exact words, writer's name. Caption: one honest question
  ("কার কথা মনে পড়ল?"). Link last.
- **Poems as carousels** (Instagram, 3-5 slides): one stanza a slide; last slide "পুরো কবিতা: hatpakha.com".
- **Story openings**: the first hook, cut at the tension. "তারপর?"

### Play 2. A word a day: আজকের শব্দ
From the word-game list (word + clue, later one line of where it comes from, from শব্দের গল্প when that section starts).
No AI cost, no invented facts. Wednesday's is the week's hardest word from the game.

### Play 3. The riddle in the feed
Morning: the day's ধাঁধা as a card, "উত্তর কমেন্টে লিখুন". Evening: the answer as a reply and a card.
Comments are the strongest signal the feeds use, and a riddle earns them honestly.

### Play 4. Players as media
- One combined daily share: "আজ হাতপাখায়: শব্দ ৫/৫ · ধাঁধা ✓ · ছক ১০০" (no spoilers).
- **আমার সপ্তাহ** card on Sundays: pieces read, days played, medal progress. One tap to share.
- Friday post: the week's top three on the leaderboard (names only of people who show their name).

### Play 5. The streak-saver and one notification a day
At most one phone notification a day per person, in this order:
1. A new episode of a serial they follow.
2. Evening, if they have a streak and have not played: "টানা ৬ দিন। আজ খেললে ৭।"
3. Otherwise the morning riddle (only for those who chose game notifications).
Two wordings are tried against each other every fortnight; the better one stays.

### Play 6. Invite milestones: হাতপাখার বন্ধু
Every signed-in reader gets an invite link. A friend who signs up and reads or plays once counts.
- 1 friend: the "বন্ধু" medal (bronze). 5: silver. 15: gold.
- 3 friends: the next serial episode one day early.
- 10 friends: their name on the monthly "হাতপাখার বন্ধু" list (if they agree).
No money, no prizes: nothing that invites cheating or needs a payment gateway.

### Play 7. Writers as our best promoters
- On publication: the e-mail carries a ready card and ready text for WhatsApp and Facebook.
- Monthly "আপনার পাঠক" note: reads, likes, the most-shared line, and a card to share.
- First-time writers get a "প্রথম লেখা" card. Nothing is more shared than a first publication.

### Play 8. WhatsApp first
- A WhatsApp Channel with one message a day at a fixed time: today's piece, today's riddle, one line.
  The text is written for the owner each morning; posting to a Channel has no automatic route, so a person pastes it
  (one minute a day).
- Every card and result on the site already has a WhatsApp button; keep it the first button everywhere.

### Play 9. Rituals and peaks
- **Weekly:** Monday পাঠকের কলম theme · Wednesday word · Friday leaderboard · Saturday serial · Sunday পাঠকের কলম picks.
- **Monthly:** the issue, the writer of the month, new medal holders.
- **Yearly peaks:** বইমেলা, ২১ ফেব্রুয়ারি, নববর্ষ, ২৫ বৈশাখ, শারদীয়া, and বর্ষসেরা with a readers' vote
  (writers campaign for votes, which brings their readers).

## 4. The week on the page (Facebook; Instagram gets the cards and carousels)

| | Morning 10:05 | Evening 19:35 | Extra |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mon | Riddle card | পাঠকের কলম theme | |
| Tue | Riddle card | এক লাইনের হাতপাখা (poem) | |
| Wed | আজকের শব্দ | Story opening | |
| Thu | Riddle card | এক লাইনের হাতপাখা | "এই দিনে" from the archive |
| Fri | Riddle card | Leaderboard top three | Serial teaser ("কাল নতুন পর্ব") |
| Sat | Serial episode | এক লাইনের হাতপাখা | |
| Sun | পাঠকের কলম picks (cards) | Writer of the week / issue | আমার সপ্তাহ prompt |

Cap: three Facebook posts and two Instagram posts a day. Riddle answers go as comments, not new posts.

## 5. The team (replaces one "marketing expert")

| Role | Job | When |
|---|---|---|
| **Marketing head** | Builds next week's calendar from the plays, the publishing schedule and last week's results | Sunday |
| **Hook writer** | Three angles for each post (a question, a line, a scene); picks one; never the same shape twice in a row | Daily |
| **Card designer** | The Hatpakha cards: line, word, riddle, leaderboard, medal, carousel slides | Daily |
| **Analyst** | Reads reach, comments, shares, clicks for every post; writes five lessons a week; the head's instructions are rewritten from them | Daily numbers, weekly lessons |
| **Community voice** | Drafts replies to comments on our posts for the owner to send (never auto-replies) | Daily |

Rules kept: only exact lines from pieces; no invented facts, quotes or numbers; no clickbait; readers' words only with
their agreement; the owner can switch any part off.

## 6. What we measure (weekly, on the marketing page)

- **Discover:** reach and shares per post, by format.
- **Return:** people who come back within 7 days; daily players; streaks of 7+.
- **Share:** shares from the site (lines, results, medals), invites accepted.
- **Convert:** new sign-ups, new পাঠকের কলম entries, new writers.
Each play gets four to six weeks. Keep what moves a number; drop what does not.

## 7. Order of building

1. **Formats and rhythm:** the cards (line, word, riddle, leaderboard), the weekly calendar, link-last posts,
   hook writer with three angles.
2. **Return:** streak-saver and one-a-day notifications, the combined daily share, আমার সপ্তাহ, the writers' share kit.
3. **Learning:** results pulled back from Facebook and Instagram, the weekly lessons, wording tests.
4. **Growth:** invite milestones and the "বন্ধু" medal; the WhatsApp daily message; later, short videos with the
   narrator's voice over a card.

## 8. Open questions and risks

- **Pictures made on the server.** Instagram needs a picture with every post. Today the cards are drawn in the
  owner's browser. Drawing Bengali text correctly on the hosting server needs checking; if it cannot be done well,
  cards are drawn whenever the marketing page is open (once a day is enough) and Instagram follows from that.
- **Facebook's and Instagram's own rules** change; the weekly lessons are how we follow them.
- **"Trending" cannot be promised.** This plan raises the chances and measures them.
- Decisions needed from the owner: how automatic; one notification a day; the rebuilt weekly e-mail (goes to the
  whole list); the invite rewards; whether a WhatsApp Channel exists or should be opened.
