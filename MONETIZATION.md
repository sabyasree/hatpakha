# Hatpakha: Monetization Plan (publishing stays free)

*Marketing-analyst view, Nov 2026. Companion to `PLAN.md`.*

## 1. The hard truth first

| Fact | Source / evidence | So what |
|---|---|---|
| Regional-language web publishers get **low ad rates** | Mint, Aug 2025: "Regional content players struggle with low ad rates, poor algorithms"; afaqs: most digital ad revenue still goes to English publishers | **AdSense alone will not pay the bills.** Treat it as a base layer, not the business. |
| Literature and entertainment have the **lowest AdSense RPMs** in India | Industry blog benchmarks (unverified): entertainment/news about ₹40–165 per 1,000 page views vs. finance ₹250–830 | Poems earn little per view. Travel, health and English pieces earn more. |
| Indian readers **do pay, but only tiny amounts, for habit-forming content** | Pratilipi: ₹9 episode unlocks, Premium about ₹149/month, about 90% of revenue from renewals; revenue about ₹86 cr FY25 yet still loss-making | Small micro-payments on serialized fiction work. Big subscriptions don't. Even the market leader isn't profitable, so don't copy their model. |
| **Durga Puja is Bengal's biggest spending season** | Puja economy estimated about ₹50,000 cr in 2025; national brands still buy Puja-annual ad pages; Sharodiya 1432 issues still sold in bookstores | One **Sharodiya issue** can earn more than the rest of the year combined. |
| **Writers are far more willing to pay than readers** | The Bengali "joint anthology" (যৌথ সংকলন) and Boi Mela self-publishing scene, where contributors buy copies and pay for publication | Charge writers for *optional extras*, never for publication itself. |

**The model, in one line:** readers are always free; writers publish free and pay only for optional extras; **brands pay to reach the audience**; and one big Sharodiya event each year.

### Rules that protect the brand (ISSN credibility)
1. **Publishing is always free and merit-based.** Paying never changes the editorial decision. Paid options only change *speed*, *depth of feedback* or *physical products*.
2. **No ads inside a poem or story body.** Ads go between sections and after the piece.
3. **Every sponsored item is labelled** "Sponsored / বিজ্ঞাপন".
4. Writers keep copyright. Any licensing deal is **opt-in with revenue share**.

## 2. Revenue streams, ranked by effort and fit

### Layer A: Free for everyone (start at launch)
| # | Stream | How | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| A1 | **AdSense, done properly** | Manual placements only (no Auto Ads), as built in D111: after the article, between "related" and comments, a wide-screen side ad; no mobile anchor ad (the bottom menu bar is there). **Lazy-load ads with reserved space** so speed and layout don't suffer. ads.txt correct. | Expect RPM to rise once the site is fast and pages get 2–3 views per visit. The **English section** (travel, health, tech, culture) and **Travel** earn the highest RPM, so they are the commercial engine. |
| A2 | **Affiliate links** | Amazon/Flipkart book links on reviews, "Boi Mela reading lists" and author books; hotel/travel affiliate links on travel pieces | Natural fit, no reader cost. |
| A3 | **Sponsored sections & native posts** | Local Bengali brands (sweets, sarees, handloom, publishers, coaching, travel agencies): "This month's Golpo section supported by …" | Sell per month, ₹3k–15k at first, rising with traffic. |
| A4 | **Newsletter + WhatsApp Channel sponsorship** | Weekly "Hatpakha Sunday" email and WhatsApp channel; one sponsor line per edition | Grows into the strongest asset because you own the audience. |

### Layer B: Writers pay small amounts for optional extras (start month 2–3)
| # | Product | Price (test) | Why writers buy |
|---|---|---|---|
| B1 | **Writing contests** (monthly theme + big ones: Sharodiya, Boi Mela, Rabindra Jayanti) | ₹49–149 entry; prizes + publication + e-certificate | Recognition. Entry fees are already normal in the Bengali literary scene. |
| B2 | **Detailed editorial feedback** | ₹99–299 per piece | AI-assisted plus a human editor's notes. Free submissions still get short feedback. |
| B3 | **Fast-track review** (48 h response instead of 7 days) | ₹49 | Speed only, never acceptance. |
| B4 | **Author certificates ("প্রকাশনা সনদ")**: free digital PDF for every published piece (with ISSN and a QR verification link). **Paid:** premium design with the editor's signature + issue cover (₹49–99), printed and framed copy shipped (₹299–499), framed poem print (₹399+) | ₹0 / ₹49–99 / ₹299–499 | Pride, sharing on Facebook/WhatsApp, and value for job and college applications. Each certificate has a public verification page, so it is credible. Contest winners get the premium version free. Print-on-demand partner, no stock. See PLAN.md §3 "Author certificates". |
| B5 | **Book publishing service** (writer's collected works → e-book + paperback, ISBN, Boi Mela launch) | ₹6,000–25,000 packages | The highest margin. Writers already pay small presses for this. |
| B6 | **Online workshops** (poetry, story craft, translation, "how to get published") | ₹299–999 | Recordings resold later. |
| B7 | **"Writer Pro" profile** (optional, later) | ₹49/month or ₹399/year | Custom profile theme, detailed read stats, priority in "Featured writers". Free profiles stay fully functional. |

### Layer C: Readers pay voluntarily (low pressure)
| # | Product | Price | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| C1 | **Sharodiya print + digital edition** | ₹250–400 pre-order | Pre-orders fund printing, so there's no risk. |
| C2 | **Tip a writer** via UPI (Razorpay) | ₹10 / ₹21 / ₹51 | The writer gets 80–90%. Builds goodwill and writers promote it themselves. |
| C3 | **Ad-free reading** | ₹29/month | Small, optional. Mainly the diaspora will buy it. |
| C4 | **Audio stories** (writers or narrators recite) | Free with ads; later premium | Bengali audio storytelling has a strong audience. |

### Layer D: Rights & partnerships (year 2+)
- **Licensing stories to audio and video platforms** (Bengali audio-story apps, short-film and web-series makers). Opt-in, shared with the writer.
- **Institutional partnerships**: colleges and schools (inter-college contests), libraries, cultural organisations, book publishers (sponsored launches).
- **Cultural grants / CSR**: Bengali-language promotion projects, translation projects.

## 3. Sharodiya: the flagship yearly event

| When | Action |
|---|---|
| May–June | Announce theme, open submissions + contest (entry fees) |
| June–July | Sell **sponsor packages**: title sponsor, section sponsors, ad pages (digital + print) |
| July–Aug | Editorial selection (AI shortlist + editors), cover art |
| Aug | **Pre-orders** open (print copies), contributor copies at a discount |
| Mahalaya | Release the digital issue free (ads + sponsors), ship print copies |
| Puja days | Daily "Puja special" stories, WhatsApp/Instagram pushes for the traffic peak |

Sponsor package idea: **Title sponsor ₹50k–1L · Section sponsor ₹10k–25k · Full-page ad ₹5k–15k · Small ad ₹1.5k–3k.** These are starting points; adjust after year 1.

## 5. Growth engine (traffic is the prerequisite for everything)

1. **Writers are the distribution channel.** Each published writer shares their piece and profile link on WhatsApp/Facebook. Make this effortless with beautiful auto-generated share cards and a "Share on WhatsApp" button, and email each writer their share kit on publication.
2. **SEO evergreen hubs** (Bengali-first): "প্রেমের কবিতা", "পুজোর গল্প", "রবীন্দ্রনাথ", "ভ্রমণ: উত্তরবঙ্গ", plus the English section for diaspora and RPM.
3. **Consistency:** at least 3 pieces per week, a monthly issue and a weekly newsletter. Google rewards a site that wakes up and stays awake.
4. **Social:** a Facebook page and groups (still the biggest for Bengali literature), Instagram reels of poem quotes (auto-generated quote cards), a WhatsApp Channel, YouTube/Spotify recitations later.
5. **Community events:** Boi Mela stall/meet-up, online kobita-path (recitation) evenings, college contests.
6. **Email capture everywhere:** "Get one story every Sunday" box after each piece.

## 6. What to build for monetization (and when)

| Phase | Build | Revenue unlocked |
|---|---|---|
| **Launch (decided)** | **AdSense** ad slots (manual, lazy, CLS-safe), ads.txt · **Contest module, free entry**: contest pages, themed submission, AI shortlist, judges' panel, results page, winner badges, e-certificate PDF · newsletter signup · sponsor banner per section | A1, A3, A4, B1 (free) |
| **When the payment gateway is approved** (Cashfree / Razorpay) | Switch on **paid contest entries** (₹49–149), paid feedback / fast-track, tips. The code is gateway-agnostic, so this is a settings toggle plus keys. | B1 (paid), B2, B3, C2 |
| Later | Affiliate link support | A2 |
| **Launch (free tier)** | Free digital publication certificate (PDF + QR verification page) for every published piece and contest entry | B4 (free) |
| +3–4 months | Tip-a-writer, workshops (event + payment + Zoom link), shop for prints/anthologies (print-on-demand), **premium and printed certificates** | B4 (paid), B6, C1, C2 |
| Before Sharodiya | Sharodiya issue builder, sponsor placements, pre-order shop, print-ready PDF | Sharodiya |
| Later | Writer Pro, ad-free membership, audio | B7, C3, C4 |

## 7. KPIs to watch monthly
Page views and **page RPM** (by section) · sessions per user · newsletter + WhatsApp subscribers · submissions · % of writers who share · contest entries · paid conversions per 100 submissions · revenue per 1,000 visitors (all streams) · cost per reviewed piece.
